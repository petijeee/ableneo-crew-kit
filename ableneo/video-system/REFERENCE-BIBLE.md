# Ableneo Video: Reference Bible

The standard we build against, distilled from deconstructing 8 world-class films
(Apple, Anthropic Cowork, Stripe, Linear, Palantir, Vercel, Buck, Territory Studio).
Read this before any video. Pair with `RUBRIC.md` (the gate).

## The 12 principles (what separates world-class from "text on a background")

1. **One idea, and it's a device, not a message.** No device = no film.
2. **Show the product doing real work.** Belief comes from substance on screen, not claims.
   *For Ableneo (a consultancy) the proof IS the product → real numbers as designed dataviz.*
3. **Type and image share ONE motion system.** Same easing, rhythm, grid. Choreographed, not layered. The system is "Lock-in": see `MOTION.md`, tokens in `knowledge/design-tokens.json` → `motion`.
4. **Sound drives the cut.** The whoosh/click *is* the transition; edit lands on the beat.
5. **Typography is designed in motion, not placed.** One typeface, engineered kerning, arrival timing.
6. **Restraint and negative space.** One line at a time, one accent color, generous silence.
7. **Abstraction is grounded in real captured texture** (real macro/light/material), never generic render.
8. **The camera moves, or nothing moves: deliberately.** Motion has weight (anticipation, overshoot, settle).
9. **Pacing has an arc.** Hook → build with proof → resolve on brand. Hold shots long enough to trust them.
10. **Brand distinctiveness is ownable and consistent.** Identifiable with the logo removed.
11. **Finish is invisible.** Color-consistent, correct motion blur, frame-accurate, zero jitter.
12. **Every element earns its place.** No idea + no substance + no craft = cut it.

## What each reference teaches us
- **Apple**: the *output* is the hero, the product hides; radical restraint; camera moves, type arrives.
  Photographic tier ("this is real"): **unreachable without real product/optics.**
- **Anthropic Cowork**: show the agent doing the real job; editorial, anti-hype; real UI + light motion.
  **Reproducible by a small team**: judgment + real product, not render budget.
- **Stripe**: one type + one color + gradient/grid motif; type and UI on ONE easing; real dashboards/data.
  The **system is the moat**, not any shot. This is our closest achievable model (data-driven).
- **Linear**: *speed is the film's physics*; stiff fast springs; cuts on the beat; real product UI.
  **Best template for a lean code-driven team.**
- **Palantir**: austerity as authority; negative-space discipline; data/maps with cartographic precision.
- **Vercel**: developer aesthetic made cinematic; code-driven geometric motion; monospace kinetic type synced to sound.
- **Buck**: authored motion craft (weight, anticipation, secondary motion) + custom sound as co-author.
  **The tier AI cannot reach**: human motion designers + scoring.
- **Territory**: ground abstraction in real captured texture so "digital" reads as physically shot.

## Honest capability map (our stack: AI gen + code motion + real data only)
- **Can hit world-class:** the Linear/Stripe/Anthropic tier IF built on real substance + one disciplined
  system + music cut to picture. For us, "real substance" = **real Ableneo numbers as designed dataviz**
  (we have no real UI to record: a deliberate constraint that caps us just below those references).
- **AI generation (Veo/Kling):** ingredient only: grounded textured b-roll ≤8s, always graded and
  integrated. **Never the backbone** (that was our amateur failure). Prefer none.
- **AI cannot give us, and we've excluded the human alternative:** authored Buck-tier motion,
  sound-to-edit lock at studio level, real product UI, the Apple photographic tier.
- **Where world-class actually lives for us:** one shared motion physics system + real-data substance +
  music-first sound. That is the whole game.

## Our realistic target
Stripe/Linear-inspired, data-driven, code-motion, sound-locked, Apple-restrained. Top-decile B2B AI
content at velocity. Not literally Apple/Buck: and we don't pretend to be.

## Levers that would raise the ceiling later (if unlocked)
1. Real screen-recordings of the product/dashboards/agent outputs (the #1 world-class signal).
2. Serious motion-craft investment or a human motion designer for hero pieces.
