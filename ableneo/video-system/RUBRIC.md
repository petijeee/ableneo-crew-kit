# Ableneo Video Rubric: the gate

Score every cut 0-10 on each dimension. **Ship only if Idea, Substance and Sound
are each ≥8, and the overall average is ≥7.** A 9 on Finish with a 2 on Idea is the
exact signature of the amateur "text on an abstract background" tier, which is what
our early attempts were. Idea, Substance and Sound are the three that separate leagues;
fail on those loops back to concept/craft, never to color/hue.

| # | Dimension | 2/10 | 6/10 | 10/10 |
|---|-----------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | **Idea / Device** | A tagline over visuals; no concept | A clear theme, mildly distinctive | One ownable device that structures the whole film (Linear = speed is the physics) |
| 2 | **Substance on screen** | Abstract loops, stock, claims only | Some real content, mostly decoration | Real proof doing real work: real numbers/dataviz that mean something, believable output |
| 3 | **Motion craft** | Linear slides, uniform fades | Competent eased animation | Authored motion with weight/anticipation/overshoot; ONE motion system for type + data (`MOTION.md`) |
| 4 | **Edit / Rhythm** | Uniform cuts ignoring music, no arc | Cuts roughly on beat, some build | Cut driven by sound; clear hook→build→resolve; shots held with intent |
| 5 | **Sound** | Stock track bolted on after picture | Fitting music, loose sync | Music chosen first, SFX *is* the transition, frame-locked to the edit |
| 6 | **Typography in motion** | Default font, fade-up, centered | One good typeface, clean placement | Engineered type system, designed arrival/kerning, animated on the film's physics |
| 7 | **Brand distinctiveness** | Generic; swap logo = any SaaS | Recognizable colors/font | Identifiable with the logo removed; an ownable motif |
| 8 | **Finish / Craft** | Flicker, off-sync, popping edges | Clean, minor tells | Invisible finish: color-consistent, correct motion blur, frame-accurate, zero jitter |

## How to use
1. Before building: does the piece have a **device** (row 1) and real **substance** (row 2)?
   If not, stop: no amount of polish saves it.
2. Choose **music first** (row 5), cut to its beats.
3. After a cut: score all 8 honestly (or have `brand-guardian` / a CD-review agent score
   adversarially). Below the gate → fix the failing high-leverage dimension, re-score.

## The amateur tells (auto-fail signals)
- Generic abstract 3D/energy loops that could sell anything.
- Claims ("80% faster") over decoration instead of over shown proof.
- Type fades/slides in generically while everything else moves differently.
- A stock track dropped on after picture-lock; cuts ignore the beat.
- Uniform ~1.5s clips, no hook, no build, no resolve.
