# Method: four pillars + business-impact first

How Ableneo delivers.

> **ESSA status:** v4 does not name ESSA. The canonical framework is the four pillars
> below. Do not use the ESSA name in new external copy until leadership confirms it still
> exists. Existing materials that mention ESSA (Lídri transformácie report, older nurture)
> stay as they are until reviewed.

## Why it exists
AI pilots in banks, telcos and insurers fail for organizational and governance reasons,
not technical ones: unclear business result, no production path, no accountability. The
method carries a use case from business case to a governed system in production.

## Business impact first
- Value before implementation scoping: KPIs are defined before delivery starts.
- The delta is reported after delivery. Measuring value is part of the culture, not a
  reporting add-on.

## The four pillars (the lens, never the invoice)
| Pillar | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Human** | AI literacy, culture, change |
| **Process** | Identify, optimize, automate |
| **Technology** | Agents, automations, apps |
| **Data** | Architecture, quality, governance |

Security is the foundation. Legal, ethics and governance are the roof: AI Act-ready trail,
human-in-the-loop by default. On the web: "one system, four layers" (homepage since
5 Oct 2026).

## How to use it in content
- Show the pillars through a real project arc, not as a diagram of jargon.
- Tie it to the anti-pilot proof (35+ of 49 AI projects run in production).
- Lead with the buyer's situation, the pillars come second.
